Privacy Policy — nihon-media-lab

Last updated: 2026-10-05

1. Use of YouTube API Services

nihon-media-lab uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
By using nihon-media-lab you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy:
http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

2. Who uses this tool

nihon-media-lab is used only by its owner, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.
It does not provide accounts to, or access data of, any other user.

3. Information we access, collect and store

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's own channels, stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Basic channel information of the owner's own channels (channel ID, title, subscriber count).
  • Videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions created by the owner, sent to YouTube when uploading.
  • YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own channels (views, watch time, subscribers), used to decide what to produce next.

No data of other YouTube users is accessed, collected or stored.

4. How information is used and shared

The information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to review the owner's channel performance.
It is not sold, and it is not shared with any third party.

5. Data retention and deletion

API data other than analytics data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
The owner can delete all stored tokens and data at any time.

6. Third-party content and advertising

nihon-media-lab does not display advertising and does not serve third-party content.

7. Cookies and device information

nihon-media-lab does not use cookies or similar technologies, and does not store information on users' devices other than the owner's own OAuth tokens.

8. Revoking access

Access granted to nihon-media-lab can be revoked at any time at:
https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
